As the set of natural numbers ℕ is comprised of 1, 2, 3 … , the numbers i, 2i, 3i … comprise the set of supernatural numbers 𝕊, and these give shape to the realm unobserved. A student of algebra, understanding it to be magic, would pay close attention indeed. This is never taught.

Duolingo indicates that *ĉiuj vi* means *you all*. In the English of the Southern US, we say 'y'all.' So, if 'ĉiuj vi' means *y'all*, then *ĉiuj vi ĉiuj* ought to mean *all y'all*, but it probably doesn't mean that.

March 15th is a good day to leave the diadem at home.

Caesar's name came to mean Emperor⁠—⁠lasting into the 20th century, as *Kaiser* and *Tsar*.
He was emulated by Napoleon and Mussolini. 
He did cross the Rubicon but could not quite end the Roman Republic as a republic.
He did loosen it, however, so others could finish what he started. 

Julius Caesar cast a long shadow⁠—⁠as did Tarquin the Proud, once upon a time.

In 2009, this satirical article was printed in New Orleans, which is home to `The National WWII Museum <https://www.nationalww2museum.org/>`_.

At that time, the second world war — global carnage and cataclysmic conclusion — 
had receded over the rear horizon into the domain of nostalgia, memorial, myth, even satire; 
and was in no way expected to appear over the forward horizon. 

`Comedy plus time equals tragedy <https://quoteinvestigator.com/2013/06/25/comedy-plus/>`_.

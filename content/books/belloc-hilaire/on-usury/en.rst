Quotes

|  **On Usury**
|  The modern world is organized on the principle that money of its nature breeds money. 
   A sum of money lent has, according to our present scheme, a natural right to interest. 
   That principle is false in economics as in morals. It ruined Rome, and it is bringing us 
   to our end.
|  — `On Usury <https://books.google.com/books?id=00YhCwAAQBAJ&lpg=PT17&vq=On%20Usury&pg=PT18>`_, Essays of a Catholic, TAN Books., Hilaire Belloc (1931)

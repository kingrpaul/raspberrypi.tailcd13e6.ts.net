When `The Book of Tea <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Book_of_Tea>`_
by `Kakuzo Okakura <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okakura_Kakuz%C5%8D>`_
appeared on `Project Gutenberg's <https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/769>`_
feed, I decided to read it. For what reason, I am not sure.
My expectations of what I would find — whatever those might have been —
were exceeded. What I did find was an author using a cup of tea as a scrying
bowl through which Western eyes might view — but, more importantly, see —
the Eastern world. This is a noble effort; its result merits reading.

I cannot with certainty judge whether the author truly understands the
Eastern world to the depth with which he seems to. However, the
artistry with which he uses the English language, I can judge,
and his writing alone makes the reading a pleasure.

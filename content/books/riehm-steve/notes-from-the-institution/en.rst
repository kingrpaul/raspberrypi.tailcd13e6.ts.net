In the late 1980s, a comic strip titled *The Institution* was published in 
`The Independent Florida Alligator <https://www.alligator.org/page/about>`_, 
a free newspaper produced for students attending the 
`University of Florida <https://www.ufl.edu/>`_.
In 1987, the cartoonist briefly self-published a collection of strips, 
including some that had not previously been printed. This collection was 
printed and sold at a local copy-shop, after which the strip was discontinued, 
most likely because the cartoonist had graduated. The quality was good. 
It is culturally significant. It seems likely that few copies are extant.

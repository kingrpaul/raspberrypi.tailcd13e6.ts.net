The 
`Evil Dead <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evil_Dead>`_
was created by 
`Sam Raimi <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Raimi>`_
and "groovy" 
`Bruce Campbell <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Campbell>`_
in 1978. It built on the 
`Lovecraft <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._P._Lovecraft>`_ 
mythos, though what Howie would have thought of it is anyone's guess. 
The franchise consists of 5 films (rebooted at #4) and a 3-season television 
series. Before *Evil Dead Rise* was released, the work had peaked in 1992 with 
`Army of Darkness <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_Darkness>`_.

Alas, this continues to be the case.

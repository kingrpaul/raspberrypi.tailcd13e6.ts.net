`Álex de la Iglesia <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_de_la_Iglesia>`_ translates 
`Lovecraft’s <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._P._Lovecraft>`_ mythos out of old New England 
and the shadow of Cotton Mather into the Vatican’s shadow in present-day 
Spain — transforming the source while remaining faithful to it. 
God bless cultural appropriation — it’s what culture is for. 
Scientologists will likely dislike what is introduced in 
Season 2 and will want to stop after Season 1.

The photography is impressive throughout, though some scenes — even 
by American standards — are not easy to watch. The title sequence 
stands on its own, even apart from the context of the show, 
and might well make `Mel Gibson <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Gibson>`_ blanch. But if you watched 
Mel’s `Passion <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Passion_of_the_Christ>`_ — and I did — without needing therapy afterward, 
you’ll likely appreciate seeing Iglesia’s interpretation.

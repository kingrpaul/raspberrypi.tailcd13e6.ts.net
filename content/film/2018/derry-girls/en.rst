One would be forgiven for imagining sectarian war to be no setting for comedy, 
an impression quickly dispelled in S1:E1, among the most humorous lengths of 
film thus far produced by western civilization—funny without being silly, 
child-focused without being childish, with uniformly standout performances 
by a regional cast.

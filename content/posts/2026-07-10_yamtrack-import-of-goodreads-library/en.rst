TL;DR: Create an account on Hardcover and import your Goodreads library there *first*.

I recently began using `Yamtrack <https://github.com/FuzzyGrim/Yamtrack>`_. I like it!

But, I had difficulties importing my .csv library from `Goodreads <https://www.goodreads.com/>`_. 

If Yamtrack does not recognize a book, the entire import may fail, the book may be skipped, or a different book with a similar title may be imported instead.

For the *arcanophile* who reads many old and obscure works, the subsequent cleanup can be tedious. And there is no bulk delete. 

Since Yamtrack uses `Hardcover <https://hardcover.app/>`_ as a source, any book that is in Hardcover's catalog should be recognized by Yamtrack.
Thus, it is helpful to have all of your books in Hardcover. For me, importing a Goodreads library into Hardcover went very smoothly.
This required creating an account, which was free and hassle-free. I didn't encounter any errors during the import, and any unknown works were added to Hardcover's library automatically. Once a book exists in Hardcover, it becomes visible to Yamtrack⁠—⁠including the obscure ones. 

In hindsight, importing my Goodreads library into Hardcover *first* would have made the subsequent import into Yamtrack much smoother.

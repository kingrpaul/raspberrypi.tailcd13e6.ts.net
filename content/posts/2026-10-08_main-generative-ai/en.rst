**10/8/2026**

   Decided to enroll — in a spririt of civic participation and to be supportive.

   Prime mover is `Kollin Napier <https://www.linkedin.com/in/kollin-napier/?isSelfProfile=false>`_.

   Administered through `Mississippi Gulf Coast Community College <https://mygulfcoast.mgccc.edu/dashboard>`_.
   Enrollment is fairly straightforward, only minor hiccups. 
   You must send an e-mail to get access to Canvas. Courses are remote and asynchronous. 

   The course **NVIDIA Generate AI** looked like the best fit. 
   Syllabus shows it's not a first introduction, is a technical course. *Bingo!*
   Non-credit, non-certificate. 
   The student is encouraged to seek two credential offered by NVIDIA. 
   I won't do that. 
   There are optional coding labs, some of which looks like I will be able to do. Python and linear algebra. 
   But, not all. Hardware limitations. That's fine. 
   Expect 25-40 hours. Seems reasonable.

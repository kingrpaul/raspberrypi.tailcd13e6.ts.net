Promotional portrait and autograph from radio personality `Doug Tracht <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_Tracht>`_ better known as The `Greaseman <https://www.greaseman.org/>`_.
This was offered with the mail-order of MP3 CDs in 1998.
The *Greasman* performed (anonymously) on AM radio station `WAPE <https://www.wape.com/>`_, "The Big Ape" in Jacksonville, Florida from 1975 until 1982. 

Produced by `Printing Impressions <https://www.printing-impressions.com/>`_ of Martinsburg, WV.
